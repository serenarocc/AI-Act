# The AI Compliance Frontier: Seminar Report

## Overview
This repository contains a report summarizing the insights from the seminar held on March 6, 2026, at **Politecnico di Torino**. 

The seminar, titled *"The AI Compliance Frontier: Balancing Innovation, Risk, and Data Protection,"* was conducted by experts from **KPMG**.

##  Key Topics
The report provides a high-level summary of the following themes discussed during the event:
* **The EU AI Act**: Analysis of the risk-based regulatory framework and its global implications.
* **Ethics and Bias**: Discussion on algorithmic fairness and real-world case studies of AI failures.
* **Data Protection**: The intersection between AI development and GDPR compliance.
* **Regulatory Enforcement**: The role of national authorities (like the Italian Garante) in monitoring AI systems.

##  Objective
The aim of this document is to outline the evolving legal and technical requirements for developing "Trustworthy AI" within the European landscape, balancing the need for innovation with the protection of fundamental rights.
